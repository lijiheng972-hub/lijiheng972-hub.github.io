Hi, I'm Jiheng.

I'm a third-year student in Intelligence Science & Technology at HUST. My research so far has one thing in common: the interesting part was rarely the model. It was the data that went into it, or the experiment that was supposed to show it worked.

On the world-model side, that meant building the pipeline that turns thousands of hours of YouTube gameplay into training clips. On the GUI-agent side, it meant finding that most of a reported gain came from the prompt around the skill, not the skill. At NC State it meant asking what a detector should do when the robot drives into a region where it can't see well.

I'm looking for research internships and RA positions in world models, multimodal agents, or robot perception, where I can keep working on questions like these.
